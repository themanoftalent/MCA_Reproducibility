# Data Availability

The numerical inputs used in this study are presented in Table 1 of the article. The supplementary Excel files, samplecounts.xlsx and 18-cell_data.xlsx, are provided separately through the journal. The repository contains the computational code, results, figures, and verification materials. The European Commission Joint Research Centre Euro 7 dataset is available from its original source at https://doi.org/10.2905/JRC.8897YXR.
